Ask Wire is a real estate data platform that exposes the Greek and Cypriot property market through a clean, developer-friendly REST API. It aggregates and structures data from multiple sources — covering everything from active sales and rental listings to court-ordered foreclosures, geospatial boundaries, automated valuations, and points of interest — so you can build market intelligence tools, investment dashboards, or customer-facing property search experiences without scraping or manual data wrangling.

<Note>
  Ask Wire is currently in **private beta**. To request access, contact the team at [support@ask-wire.com](mailto:support@ask-wire.com) with a brief description of your use case. The team will respond with credentials and onboarding details.
</Note>

## Who Ask Wire is for

Ask Wire is designed for:

- **Proptech developers** building search portals, investment tools, or market analytics products focused on Greece or Cyprus.
- **Financial institutions and funds** that need structured foreclosure and valuation data for due diligence or portfolio analysis.
- **Real estate agencies** looking to integrate live market data into their internal tools or client-facing platforms.
- **Researchers and analysts** who need reliable, machine-readable property data for academic or market research.

## How the API works

All Ask Wire endpoints follow REST conventions and return JSON. Authentication uses JWT Bearer tokens obtained from the authentication service at `zeus.ask-wire.com`. All property, geocoding, GIS, and valuation endpoints are served from `pandora.ask-wire.com`. Tokens expire every 24 hours and must be refreshed by re-authenticating. See the [Authentication guide](/authentication) and the [Quickstart](/quickstart) to get your first request working in minutes.
